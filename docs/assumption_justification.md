## Revenue growth %

**Historical:** FY2021: n/a, FY2022: 10.6%, FY2023: 12.0%, FY2024: 15.2%, FY2025: 13.0%

- **Base:** 13.0% (2026) -> 7.0% (2030). Starts near the FY24-25 average growth rate (~14.1%), decelerating to 7% by year 5 - typical maturation curve for a scaling DTC retailer, consistent with WRBY's own deceleration from 15.2% (FY24) to 13.0% (FY25).
- **Best:** 15.0% (2026) -> 11.0% (2030). Holds near FY24's peak growth rate (15.2%) and decelerates more slowly - new store productivity and category expansion (contacts, exams) sustain growth longer.
- **Worst:** 8.0% (2026) -> 3.0% (2030). Growth roughly halves from the FY25 rate immediately - reflects competitive/macro pressure on discretionary eyewear spend, decelerating to GDP-like growth by year 5.

- **Worst:** 53.5% (2026) -> 51.0% (2030). Continued compression from FY21's 58.8% peak - input cost inflation and promotional intensity outpace efficiency gains.
